## Features

### Paladin
- **Lay on Hands** (1): Bonus Action, heal from a 30 HP pool, or spend 5 to cure Poisoned.
- **Spellcasting** (1): Charisma, prepared caster.
- **Weapon Mastery** (1): two weapon kinds. Warhammer *(Push)* + one more *(ask DM)*.
- **Fighting Style: Blessed Warrior** (2): Sacred Flame and Thaumaturgy as Paladin cantrips.
- **Paladin's Smite** (2): Divine Smite always prepared, one free cast per Long Rest.
- **Channel Divinity** (3): 2 uses, shared by Divine Sense and Vow of Enmity.
- **Divine Sense** (3): Bonus Action, sense Celestials, Fiends and Undead within 60 ft for 10 minutes.
- **Ability Score Improvement** (4): *(ask DM what was taken)*.
- **Extra Attack** (5): attack twice per Attack action.
- **Faithful Steed** (5): Find Steed always prepared, one free cast per Long Rest.
- **Aura of Protection** (6): +3 to saves for you and allies within 10 ft.

### Oath of Vengeance
- **Vow of Enmity** (3): with the Attack action, spend 1 Channel Divinity for Advantage on attacks against one creature within 30 ft for 1 minute. It transfers if that creature drops to 0 HP.
- *Coming at 7:* **Relentless Avenger**. When you hit with an Opportunity Attack, the target's Speed drops to 0 and you move half your Speed.

### Human
- **Resourceful:** Heroic Inspiration after every Long Rest.
- **Skillful:** one extra skill proficiency.
- **Versatile:** an Origin feat *(ask DM which)*.

### Feats
- **Crafter** (Origin): three Artisan's Tools, 20% discount on nonmagical items, Fast Crafting after a Long Rest.
- **Blessed Warrior** (Fighting Style).
