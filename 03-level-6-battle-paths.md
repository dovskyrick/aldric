# Aldric Sonet — Level 6 Battle Paths

> A decision guide for a level 6 Oath of Vengeance Paladin. The idea: **declare the Vow, keep the aura over your friends, and spend the bonus action wisely.**

---

## Resources per long rest (tick these off)

| Resource | Amount | Comes back |
|---|---|---|
| 1st-level slots | 4 | Long rest |
| 2nd-level slots | 2 | Long rest |
| Free Divine Smite (Paladin's Smite) | 1 | Long rest |
| Free Find Steed (Faithful Steed) | 1 | Long rest |
| Channel Divinity (Vow / Divine Sense) | 2 | 1 on short rest, all on long rest |
| Lay on Hands pool | 30 HP | Long rest |
| Emberthread Bandage | 6 uses | Doesn't come back |
| Potion of Healing | 2 | Doesn't come back |

## Before the fight (round 0)

### Summon the steed early
Use the free Find Steed **outside combat**. It takes an Action but lasts until it's killed. Arriving with a 60-ft mount or an extra body on the field is a free advantage.

### Pre-cast if you get a warning
If you hear enemies coming, cast **Hunter's Mark** or **Shield of Faith** before initiative. Hunter's Mark lasts an hour, and now that concentration rolls are +5 it holds up well.

### Plan your aura formation
Before the door opens, line the party up so the squishiest allies are within **10 ft of you**. You're the anchor, so walk in the middle, not in front.

## Round 1: pick your path

### Path A — Single big threat (boss / brute)
**Action:** Attack and declare the **Vow** on the boss. **Bonus:** **Hunter's Mark** on it. From then on you swing with Advantage for 1d8+6+1d6 per hit.

### Path B — Humanoid boss or leader
**Action:** **Hold Person** (2nd-level slot, DC 14). If it fails, every melee hit you land next round is an **automatic crit**. Keep the Vow for next round, since paralysis already gives you Advantage.

### Path C — Many weak enemies
**Action:** Attack (no Vow, the targets die too fast). **Bonus:** Hunter's Mark and move it with a bonus action when the target drops. Spread the Push mastery around and save your Channel Divinity.

### Path D — Enemy spellcasters or save-heavy monsters
**Action:** **Bless** yourself plus two allies near you. Inside your aura they get **+3 +1d4 on saves**, which shuts down fear, charm and breath attacks. This is level 6's strongest combo.

### Path E — Undead or fiends
**Bonus (before moving):** Divine Sense if they're hidden. Otherwise attack with the Vow and **Smite on hit**: +1d8 extra radiant against them. Favour smites over Hunter's Mark here.

### Path F — The party is losing
**Bonus:** Lay on Hands on whoever is lowest, or the person who's down. **Move** so the aura covers the most allies. **Action:** attack, or Dodge if you're the one being focused.

## Rounds 2+: keep it going

### Hunter's Mark already running
Your bonus action is free. **Smite on a crit**, or smite when the target is close to dying. Otherwise keep it for Lay on Hands emergencies.

### Vowed target dies
Move the Vow to a new creature within 30 ft at **no cost**. Hunter's Mark can move too, but that takes a bonus action on a later turn. Do the Vow first, then decide about the mark.

### Paralyzed target (from Path B)
Get within 5 ft. Both hits are automatic crits: **2 × (2d8+6)**. Smite one of them: a 1st-level slot adds 4d8 and a 2nd-level slot adds 6d8, all doubled. That's the biggest damage you can do.

### Concentration broke
Don't panic. Ask: is the fight still long? Recast Hunter's Mark (bonus). Is the party struggling with saves? Switch to Bless (action). Only one runs at a time.

### Low on slots
Use the **free Paladin's Smite** first, then 1st-level slots. Save the 2nd-level slots for Hold Person or emergency Misty Step. Lay on Hands costs no slots at all.

## Bonus action priority list (top wins)

### 1. Someone is at 0 HP
Lay on Hands, even for just 1 HP. A downed ally loses their whole turn. Getting them up beats any amount of damage.

### 2. Natural 20 on an attack
Divine Smite immediately. The crit doubles all smite dice. Use your highest slot if the target matters.

### 3. Long fight, no concentration running
Hunter's Mark on the main target. Over 3 rounds of two hits each, the +1d6 per hit (≈21 damage) beats one 1st-level smite (≈9).

### 4. A hit that finishes a dangerous enemy
Smite to secure the kill when the enemy is nearly dead. One less enemy acting is worth more than saving a slot.

### 5. Need to reach someone
Misty Step (2nd-level slot) when 30+ ft of terrain or enemies are in the way. It doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

### 6. Nothing urgent
A bonus-action potion if you're hurt, or Shield of Faith if you're not concentrating. Never waste the bonus action without checking these.

## Full example rounds

### Example 1 — Boss fight (Zrrquel the general?)
**R1:** Vow + attack ×2, Hunter's Mark. **R2:** attack ×2, smite on a crit. **R3:** Bless if their attacks call for saves. Stay within 10 ft of the healer.

### Example 2 — Ambush in Dawn Wood
**R1:** Move so 2–3 allies are in the aura, attack without the Vow, Hunter's Mark. **R2+:** move the mark between kills and use Push to keep enemies off the casters.

### Example 3 — A human cultist leader
**R1:** Hold Person. **R2:** walk up, Vow + attack ×2 (auto-crits), smite one with a 1st-level slot. The leader usually goes down in a single turn.

### Example 4 — Friend poisoned and bleeding
**Bonus:** Lay on Hands for 5 to end Poisoned, or 5 + more to heal too (these are separate spends). **Action:** attack. Use a bandage for blindness or paralysis.

## Common mistakes to avoid

### Two slots in one turn
Bless (slot) followed by Smite (slot) is **not allowed** in 2024. Bless with a slot plus the *free* Paladin's Smite **is** allowed.

### Smite + Hunter's Mark in one turn
Both use the bonus action, so you only get one. Cast the mark in round 1, then smite on later rounds.

### Wandering off alone
At level 6 your best defence is the aura. If you run 30 ft ahead, nobody gets +3 saves. Charge only when the steed can bring you back.

### Forgetting your own aura
Add **+3 to your own saves** too. Before rolling a save, say "Aura of Protection" out loud so nobody forgets.

### Rummaging in the backpack mid-fight
Equipping items from the backpack takes your **whole turn** at your table. Keep potions and bandages equipped and swap gear before combat.
