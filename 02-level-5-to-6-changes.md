# Aldric Sonet — What Changed from Level 5 to Level 6

> Paladin 6, Oath of Vengeance, D&D 2024. Short answer: **level 6 gives one big feature, Aura of Protection, plus more HP.** Everything else stays the same.

---

## What's new at level 6

### More Hit Points
Roll 1d10 + 2 (Con), or take the average of 8. The Alchemy sheet now shows **58 max HP** (it was 50). Hit Dice go from 5d10 to **6d10** for short rests.

### Bigger Lay on Hands pool
The pool is always 5 × Paladin level, so it grows from 25 to **30 HP** per long rest. Cleansing poison still costs 5 from it.

### ⭐ Aura of Protection (the big one)
You give off an invisible **10-foot aura**. **You and every ally inside it add +3 (your Charisma modifier) to ALL saving throws.** No action and no concentration. It's always on.

### How big is 10 feet?
On a grid that's **2 squares in every direction** from you, a 5×5 square block with you in the centre. Allies need to end up inside it when the enemy makes them save.

### When the aura shuts off
It goes inactive while you are **Incapacitated**: unconscious, stunned, paralyzed and so on. It doesn't help your own death saves. If you go down, the whole party loses the bonus.

### Your new saving throws
With the aura you get: **Str +7, Dex +3, Con +5, Int +2, Wis +7, Cha +9**. Your weakest saves are now playable and your strongest are close to unbeatable.

### Concentration is much safer now
Concentration checks are Con saves, so you now roll **+5 instead of +2**. Hunter's Mark, Bless and Hold Person last far longer when you take hits.

### Aura + Bless
An ally who is blessed and inside your aura adds **+3 and +1d4** to every save. Against fear, charm or dragon breath your group is now very hard to break.

### Allies' death saves
Death saving throws are saving throws, so a downed ally within 10 ft of you gets **+3 on death saves**. Stand next to a fallen friend while you fight.

### If there's another paladin
A creature can benefit from only one Aura of Protection at a time and picks which. Two paladins don't stack, so spread out to cover more people.

## What did NOT change at level 6

### Proficiency bonus
Still **+3**. It goes to +4 at level 9. Weapon attacks (+9 with DuskMare), Spell DC 14 and spell attack +6 stay the same.

### Spell slots
Still **4 first-level and 2 second-level slots**, regained on a long rest. Level 7 adds a third second-level slot.

### Prepared spells
Still **6 chosen spells**, plus your always-prepared Oath spells (Bane, Hunter's Mark, Hold Person, Misty Step), Divine Smite and Find Steed. You can swap chosen spells after a long rest.

### Channel Divinity
Still **2 uses**: 1 back on a short rest, all back on a long rest. Shared between Vow of Enmity and Divine Sense. It goes to 3 at level 11.

## Level 5 things worth reviewing (still fresh)

### Extra Attack
You attack **twice** whenever you take the Attack action. Both swings get the Vow's Advantage and Hunter's Mark damage, and either one can trigger a smite.

### Faithful Steed (from the session notes: "this one is important")
Cast **Find Steed once per long rest with no slot**. Casting takes an Action and the steed lasts until it drops to 0 HP, so **summon it before combat**, ideally at the start of the day.

### The Otherworldly Steed (free cast = 2nd level)
A Large spirit mount. Check these numbers with the DM's stat block: about **AC 12, HP 25, speed 60 ft**, and a Slam at **+6 for 1d8+2**. You pick Celestial, Fey or Fiend.

### Steed type choice
**Celestial:** heals a creature (Healing Touch). **Fey:** teleports with its rider (Fey Step). **Fiend:** frightens with a glare (Fell Glare). Celestial suits a support-heavy paladin best.

### Riding in combat
Mounting or dismounting costs half your movement. A mount with 60 ft speed lets you charge across the map and still attack twice. Ask the DM how the steed acts while you ride it.

### Second-level spells
**Hold Person** (paralyze a humanoid, melee hits become crits) and **Misty Step** (30 ft teleport, bonus action). Both are Oath spells, so they're always prepared.

## Coming next: level 7 preview

### Relentless Avenger (Oath of Vengeance)
When you hit with an **opportunity attack**, the target's speed drops to 0 and you can move half your speed as part of the reaction without provoking. Fleeing enemies get stuck.

### More resources at 7
You gain a third 2nd-level slot and 7 prepared spells. Lay on Hands grows to 35.
