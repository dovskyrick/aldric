# Aldric Sonet — Typical Paladin Turn Workflows

> Oath of Vengeance Paladin, D&D 2024 rules. Numbers come from the Alchemy sheet (the DM's version).
> Quick stats: **DuskMare +9 to hit, 1d8+6** · **Spell DC 14** · **Spell attack +6** · **AC 18** · **Speed 30 ft**

---

## Part 1 — What a turn is made of

### Your turn budget
Every turn you get **one Action, one Bonus Action, movement (30 ft)** and **one Reaction** per round. You can split movement before and after attacks. Hold onto this list.

### Free object interaction
You get one free small interaction per turn: draw a weapon, open a door, pick up an item. Digging through the backpack takes your whole turn at this table, so keep healing items equipped.

### The bonus action is the bottleneck
Hunter's Mark, Divine Smite, Lay on Hands, Shield of Faith, Misty Step and Divine Sense **all use your bonus action**. Pick one per turn. Most turns come down to this choice.

### One spell slot per turn (2024 rule)
In 2024 you can spend **only one spell slot per turn**. Bless with a slot plus Smite with a slot can't happen in the same turn. The free Paladin's Smite doesn't use a slot.

### Concentration: one at a time
Bless, Bane, Hunter's Mark, Shield of Faith, Hold Person and Protection from Evil all need concentration. Starting a new one ends the old one. When you take damage, make a Con save (DC 10 or half the damage).

### Vow of Enmity costs no action
The Vow is **part of the Attack action**. Say it as you start attacking: spend 1 Channel Divinity and get **Advantage on every attack** against that target for 1 minute.

### Correction to the session notes
The notes say "we only attack the first one with advantage." In fact the Vow gives Advantage on **all** your attacks against the vowed creature for the full minute, both swings every turn.

## Part 2 — Common workflows

### 1. Boss opener: Vow + Hunter's Mark
**Action:** Attack and declare the Vow on the boss. **Bonus:** Hunter's Mark the same target. Every hit after that deals 1d8+6 +1d6 with Advantage. Use this for long fights.

### 2. Burst: Vow + Divine Smite
**Action:** Attack with the Vow. **Bonus (only after a hit):** Divine Smite for +2d8 radiant (3d8 at a 2nd-level slot). You choose after you see the hit, so a smite never misses.

### 3. Fishing for crits
Advantage from the Vow roughly doubles your crit chance. On a natural 20, **smite right away**: all the smite dice double. A level-1 slot then deals 4d8 radiant.

### 4. Hunter's Mark round 1, Smite later?
Hunter's Mark keeps paying out (+1d6 per hit) while you hold concentration. Once it's up, your bonus action is free again, so you can smite on later turns with a slot.

### 5. Support opener: Bless
**Action:** cast Bless on yourself and two allies. They add +1d4 to attacks and saves. You lose your attacks this turn, so it's best when the party is big or the fight will be hard.

### 6. Debuff opener: Bane
**Action:** Bane on up to three enemies (Cha save, DC 14). The ones who fail subtract 1d4 from their attacks and saves. Use it against a group of strong hitters. It takes concentration, so it replaces Hunter's Mark.

### 7. Lockdown: Hold Person
**Action:** Hold Person (2nd-level slot, DC 14 Wis) on a humanoid. A paralyzed target within 5 ft takes **automatic crits** from melee hits. Next turn, smite those crits for huge damage.

### 8. Tank turn: Shield of Faith
**Bonus:** Shield of Faith on yourself (AC 20) or on a fragile ally. **Action:** attack normally. It takes concentration, so only use it when nothing better is running.

### 9. Medic turn: Lay on Hands + attack
**Bonus:** Lay on Hands on a hurt ally (30 HP pool) and spend exactly what they need. **Action:** still attack twice. Healing and damage in the same turn is what makes a paladin strong.

### 10. Ally down: get them up
Even **1 HP** from Lay on Hands gets an unconscious ally back up. Move to them, use your bonus action to heal, then attack whatever is nearby. Save the bulk of the pool for this.

### 11. Cleanse a condition
Poisoned? Lay on Hands for 5 points ends it (bonus action). Blinded, deafened or paralyzed? Use the Emberthread Bandage (action, 2d4+2) or Lesser Restoration (bonus action in 2024, 2nd-level slot).

### 12. Drinking potions (house rule)
**Action:** drink the whole Potion of Healing for a flat 10 HP. **Bonus action:** gulp it for 2d4+2 and still attack. When you're in trouble but can still swing, take the bonus-action version.

### 13. Gap closer: Misty Step
**Bonus:** Misty Step to teleport 30 ft, which avoids opportunity attacks and difficult terrain. **Action:** attack whoever you land next to. You can't smite this turn because your bonus action is spent.

### 14. Out of reach: ranged fallback
Can't reach the target? Cast **Sacred Flame** (60 ft, Dex save DC 14, 2d8 radiant) or throw a javelin if you carry them. Never end a turn doing nothing.

### 15. Battlefield control: Shove or Grapple
Swap one of your two attacks for a Shove (knock Prone or push 5 ft) or a Grapple (DC 15). A prone enemy gives your allies Advantage on melee attacks.

### 16. Push mastery positioning
Warhammer mastery **Push**: a hit shoves a Large or smaller target 10 ft away. Use it to push enemies off ledges, into hazards, or away from a downed ally.

### 17. Reaction: opportunity attacks
If an enemy leaves your reach, use your reaction for one melee attack. In 2024 Divine Smite is a bonus action, and those only happen on your own turn, so you can't smite here.

### 18. Undead and fiends
Use **Divine Sense** (bonus, 1 Channel Divinity) to find them. Divine Smite deals **+1d8 extra** against undead and fiends. Spend slots on them first.

### 19. Channel Divinity budget
You have **2 uses**. You get 1 back on a short rest and all of them on a long rest. Vow and Divine Sense share the pool, so save it for the Vow unless you're hunting undead.

### 20. Turn checklist
1) Is anyone down? 2) Is my concentration spell running? 3) Do I declare the Vow? 4) Attack, attack. 5) Bonus action: smite, mark or heal? 6) Where do I end my move?
