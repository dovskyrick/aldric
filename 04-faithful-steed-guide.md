# Aldric Sonet — Faithful Steed & Mounted Combat Guide

> Level 5 feature you're picking up now at level 6. D&D 2024 rules. Stat-block numbers assume the **free cast (2nd-level spell)**. Confirm table rulings with the DM.

---

## Part 1 — Steed mechanics

### What Faithful Steed gives you
You always have **Find Steed** prepared, and once per long rest you can cast it **without a spell slot**. After that, casting it again costs a 2nd-level slot. You only have two of those, so the free cast is your main option.

### Casting it
Casting takes **one Action**, range 30 ft. The spell is Instantaneous: no concentration, and the steed **stays until it drops to 0 HP or you die**. Cast it at the start of the adventuring day, not in the middle of a fight.

### What it looks like
A **Large** rideable animal of your choice, like a horse, elk, dire wolf or camel. It's an otherworldly spirit, so the look is just flavour. Pick something that fits Aldric and DuskMare. A dark warhorse matches the weapon nicely.

### Its stats at the free cast
**AC 12, HP 25, Speed 60 ft.** Str 18, Dex 12, Con 14. Attack: **Otherworldly Slam, +6 to hit (your spell attack), 1d8+2** damage. Flying requires a 4th-level slot, which is many levels away for you.

### Choosing its type
Each cast you pick **Celestial, Fey or Fiend**. That decides the Slam's damage type and gives one special bonus action, usable **once per long rest**. You can pick differently every time you re-summon it.

### Celestial: Healing Touch
Slam deals **Radiant** damage. Bonus action: one creature within 5 ft of the steed regains **2d8+2 HP**. That's an extra heal that doesn't spend your bonus action or your Lay on Hands pool. This is the default pick for a support-minded paladin.

### Fey: Fey Step
Slam deals **Psychic** damage. Bonus action: the steed **teleports up to 60 ft, rider included**, to a space it can see. It's a great escape or gap-closer: over a chasm, past a wall of enemies, or out of a grapple-heavy mess.

### Fiend: Fell Glare
Slam deals **Necrotic** damage. Bonus action: one creature within 60 ft makes a **Wis save against DC 14** or is **Frightened** until the end of your next turn. Frightened enemies have Disadvantage on attacks and can't move closer.

### Life Bond
Whenever **you** regain HP from a **level 1+ spell** while within 5 ft of the steed, the steed regains the same amount. Cure Wounds counts. Lay on Hands, potions and bandages are **not** spells, so they don't trigger it.

### Initiative and turns
The steed **shares your initiative count**. While you ride it, it's a *controlled mount* (see Part 2). If you become **Incapacitated**, it takes its turn right after yours and acts on its own, focusing on protecting you.

### When it disappears
At **0 HP** or if **you die**, it vanishes and drops whatever it carried. Recasting summons the same steed or a new one. Casting while one already exists **replaces** it. Don't waste a recast on that.

### Getting around
A Large steed takes up **2×2 squares** and may not fit narrow tunnels or small doorways. Outdoors (Dawn Wood roads, open fields) it's strong. Inside cramped dungeons, sometimes leave it outside guarding the camp or the gear.

## Part 2 — Mounting mechanics

### Mounting and dismounting
During your move you can mount or dismount a willing creature **within 5 ft** that's at least one size larger. Each time costs **half your Speed: 15 ft** of your own movement. If you don't have 15 ft left, you can't do it this turn.

### Whose speed do you use?
While mounted you **move on the steed's 60 ft**, not your own 30 ft. Your own movement only pays for mounting or dismounting. In practice you can mount, then travel 60 ft (or 120 ft with Dash) in the same turn.

### Controlled mount: what it can do
While you ride it, the steed moves where you direct and its action can **only be Dash, Disengage or Dodge**. It **can't Slam while ridden**. Ask your DM whether its special bonus action works while ridden (Fey Step's wording says yes).

### Your actions while riding
Riding costs **none of your actions**. You still get your full Attack action (two swings with the Vow), your bonus action (smite, Hunter's Mark, Lay on Hands) and your reaction. The steed just gives you mobility and its own action.

### Splitting movement around attacks
You can split the steed's movement before and after your attacks: ride in 30 ft, swing twice, ride out 30 ft. If the steed takes **Disengage**, nobody gets an opportunity attack on the way out.

### Opportunity attacks against you
If the mount provokes an opportunity attack, the attacker can target **either you or the steed**. With only 25 HP and AC 12, the steed is the easier target. Disengage protects both of you.

### Falling off
If you're knocked **Prone**, or the steed is moved against its will, make a **DC 10 Dex save** or fall off and land Prone within 5 ft. You make the same save if the steed itself is knocked Prone.

### Unmounted steed
When you aren't riding it, the steed takes its own turn on your initiative and can **Slam (+6, 1d8+2)**, move 60 ft and use its bonus action. It's like having an extra party member. Ask your DM how directly you control it.

### Aura of Protection while mounted
Your 10 ft aura still comes from **you**. A Large mount pushes you into the middle of a bigger footprint, so check that allies are really within 10 ft of your position before you charge off.

## Part 3 — Turn workflows with the steed

### Workflow 1: Hit-and-run cavalry charge (mounted)
**Steed's action:** Disengage. **Move:** ride 30 ft to the target. **Your action:** Attack ×2 with the Vow. **Bonus:** Hunter's Mark or Smite. **Move:** ride 30 ft back to the party. No opportunity attacks, the target is damaged, and your aura is back on your allies.

### Workflow 1 — Round-by-round
**R1:** Vow + Hunter's Mark, then pull back. **R2:** charge in again, attack twice, smite on a crit, return. A boss that has to walk to you now takes opportunity attacks, and Relentless Avenger at level 7 makes this deadly.

### Workflow 2: Two bodies on the field (unmounted)
**You:** fight on foot with the Vow, Attack ×2, and Smite or Lay on Hands as your bonus action. **Steed:** guards the squishiest ally or the downed friend, Slams adjacent enemies (+6, 1d8+2), and holds Healing Touch (Celestial) as an emergency heal.

### Workflow 2 — When to switch
Start unmounted to get the extra Slam and an extra body for the enemy to target. If a fight drags on, or you need to reach a distant caster, spend 15 ft to mount and switch to Workflow 1 for speed and Disengage.

### Bonus emergency trick: Fey Step rescue
If you summoned it as **Fey**: an ally is surrounded or you're trapped. Mount up, and the steed's bonus action Fey Steps you **60 ft** away. Then use Lay on Hands on yourself or your ally. Use it once per long rest, when it really matters.
